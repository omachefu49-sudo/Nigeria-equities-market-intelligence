# Nigerian Equities Market Intelligence

**Project Status:** 🟡 Planned

An independent financial market research and data analytics project investigating the Nigerian equities market, focusing on corporate financial performance, valuation, financial resilience and investment opportunities.

## 1. Problem Statement

Investors often struggle to distinguish companies with strong underlying financial fundamentals from those experiencing temporary market appreciation.

Share-price movements alone do not adequately reflect corporate financial health, earnings sustainability, liquidity or valuation attractiveness.

This project seeks to bridge this information gap through systematic financial and quantitative analysis of publicly available market data.

## 2. Research Objectives

- Evaluate the financial performance of selected NGX-listed companies.
- Assess profitability, liquidity, leverage and earnings quality.
- Examine corporate valuation using relevant financial multiples.
- Investigate dividend performance and sustainability.
- Explore relationships between macroeconomic conditions and equity performance.
- Identify financially resilient companies and potential investment opportunities.

## 3. Data Sources

- [Nigerian Exchange Group (NGX)](https://ngxgroup.com/)
- [Securities and Exchange Commission Nigeria](https://sec.gov.ng/)
- Individual listed companies' investor relations websites.
- [Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN)](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/)
- [National Bureau of Statistics (NBS)](https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/)

## 4. Analytical Approach

- Financial statement and ratio analysis.
- Comparative company and sector analysis.
- Time-series analysis.
- Corporate valuation assessment.
- Dividend sustainability analysis.
- Correlation and sensitivity analysis.
- Financial risk assessment and benchmarking.
